# PolarOS

A tiny OS that runs in your browser, built for fun hehe.

I made this for the **WebOS** mission of [Stardance](https://stardance.hackclub.com/missions/web-os-1) by Hack Club. The idea was to build my own little operating system and to make it look like *mine*. I really like the style of Nothing (the brand), so I went for black and white, a dot grid everywhere, a dot-matrix font and a bit of dark red.

---

## What it does

### The desktop
- A dot grid background with a mountain hiding behind it
- App icons on the left
- Click an icon to select it, double click to open the app
- A bottom bar with the name of the OS and a live clock

### The windows
- You can move every window by grabbing its title
- The little red dot closes it
- The window you click on comes in front of the others, like a real OS

### Welcome
The first thing you see. It says hi, shows my mascot (isn't he beautiful..?) and has a link to my Instagram. If you come from Stardance, text me, it will put a smile on my face hehe

### Notes
- Write as many notes as you want
- Change the title and the text of each note
- Delete the ones you don't need anymore
- **Your notes stay saved** even if you close or reload the page (they're stored in your browser). This is the feature I added that isn't in the guide :)

### Pong
- **Auto** mode: the computer plays against itself, you can just watch (it's weirdly relaxing)
- **1 Player** mode: you're the paddle on the left, move your mouse over the game to play
- The computer is a bit slow on purpose so it misses sometimes, so you actually have a chance
